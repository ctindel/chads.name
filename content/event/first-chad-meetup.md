## ChadCon 2026

The moment you've been waiting for: the **first-ever ChadCon** is happening in Austin, Texas.

### What to Expect

ChadCon is a three-day event designed for Chads, by Chads. Here's what's on the agenda:

- **Friday Evening** — Welcome reception and icebreaker. Meet Chads from across the country in a casual setting.
- **Saturday** — Full day of activities including panel discussions, networking sessions, outdoor adventures, and the legendary Chad BBQ.
- **Sunday Morning** — Farewell brunch and planning session for ChadCon 2027.

### Who Can Attend

ChadCon is open to **verified members only**. If you haven't verified your name yet, head to the [Verify Your Name](/verify/) page to get started.

### Registration

Early bird registration opens soon. Subscribe to the [newsletter](/newsletter/) to be the first to know when tickets go on sale.

### Location

Austin, Texas was chosen for its legendary food scene, outdoor activities, and central location. Venue details will be announced closer to the event.
