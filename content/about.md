## What is ChadCon?

ChadCon is the world's first and only private social group exclusively for people whose legal first name is **Chad**.

We started with a simple question: *What if every Chad in the world could connect?* The result is a verified, exclusive community where every member shares the most legendary name in history.

### Why We Exist

The name Chad has become iconic in internet culture — but behind the memes are real people. Doctors, engineers, teachers, athletes, artists, entrepreneurs. People who share more than a name — they share a bond.

ChadCon exists to turn that bond into something real. We host events, build connections, and create a space where being named Chad is celebrated.

### How It Works

1. **Verify your name** using ID.me, a trusted identity verification platform
2. **Join the community** on Discord, where verified Chads connect daily
3. **Attend events** — from local meetups to the annual ChadCon gathering
4. **Subscribe to the newsletter** for event announcements and community news

### Our Values

- **Authenticity** — Every member is verified. No aliases, no exceptions.
- **Brotherhood** — We support each other. The Chad network is real.
- **Fun** — Life's too short not to celebrate sharing the best name ever.
- **Inclusivity** — Every Chad is welcome, regardless of background.
