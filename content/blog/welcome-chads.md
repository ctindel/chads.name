Today we're proud to launch **ChadCon** — the world's first and only private social group exclusively for people whose legal first name is Chad.

## Why Now?

The name Chad has taken on a life of its own in internet culture. But behind every meme is a real person — and there are a lot of us. According to Social Security Administration data, Chad has been a popular name in the United States since the 1970s, with hundreds of thousands of Chads walking among us.

We thought it was time those Chads had a place to connect.

## What We're Building

ChadCon is more than a novelty. We're building a verified, exclusive community with:

- **ID.me verification** to ensure every member is a genuine Chad
- **Regional meetups** across the country
- **ChadCon** — our annual gathering of Chads
- **A newsletter** to keep the community connected
- **A Discord server** for daily conversation and networking

## How to Join

Getting started is simple:

1. Visit our [Verify Your Name](/verify/) page
2. Complete the ID.me verification process
3. Subscribe to the [newsletter](/newsletter/) for event updates
4. Join the Discord (link provided after verification)

## The Road Ahead

This is just the beginning. We have big plans for 2026 and beyond — more events, more cities, more Chads. If your name is Chad, this is your community.

Welcome to the brotherhood.
